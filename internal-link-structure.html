1 • Executive summary & current state

An internal link is any <a>/<Link> from one page on the domain to another page on the same domain. Internal links do three jobs: (1) help Google discover & crawl pages, (2) pass authority (PageRank) from strong pages to money pages, and (3) tell Google what a page is about via the anchor text. Right now the site has a strong top-down mega menu but three structural gaps that leak ranking potential.

Finding 1 — Footer passes ZERO internal link equity. components/Footer.tsx only renders links to other JKKN domains (ahs, pharmacy, nursing…) and social profiles (with rel="nofollow"). Every page on the site has a footer, so a footer “Quick Links” block is the single highest-leverage place to flow authority to admissions/BDS/MDS/fees.
Finding 2 — The actual conversion pages are missing from the Admission menu. navigationMenuLink.ts → Admission submenu lists Prospectus, Fee-Refund, Scholarship, SEDG — but NOT /admissions/bds/, /admissions/mds/ or /admission-process/, which are the real lead pages. Two existing nav links (/admissions/admission-process-guidelines/…) also 404.
Finding 3 — High-value pages are near-orphans. /placements/, /fees-structure/, /scholarships/, /testimonials/, /iqac/, /dental-surgery-assistant-course/ and the 5 city pages are NOT in the mega menu and NOT in the footer — they survive on a handful of scattered in-body links. Orphan/near-orphan pages crawl slowly and rank poorly.
What is already good. The mega menu (Header) gives deep top-down coverage of About / Academics / Accreditation / Research / Committee / Facilities. The 5 city pages already cross-link each other and point to fees + placements — a working geo-cluster that just needs connecting to the global nav.

5 • Global linking layers

Internal links should come from four layers. The site currently uses only one well (header).

LayerStatusRole & recommendation
1. Header mega menuStrongTop-down crawl path to ~130 pages. Fix: add /admissions/bds/, /admissions/mds/, /admission-process/ to the Admission group; remove the two 404 admission-process-guidelines links.
2. Footer quick-linksMissingAdd a sitewide “Quick Links” column: Admissions, BDS, MDS, Fees, Scholarships, Placements, Facilities, Contact. This single block lifts every near-orphan in one shot and appears on all ~150 pages.
3. Contextual (in-body)PartialThe highest-value links for ranking — keyword-rich, in running text. Build out per the §4 matrix. Programs↔blog↔admissions↔fees are the priority bridges.
4. Related-content blocksOptionalA “Related pages” / “You may also like” module at the bottom of program, blog and city pages to automate sibling + cross-cluster links.
Breadcrumbs are NOT a linking layer here. Per the project rule, only “Home” is clickable in any breadcrumb; all other crumbs are plain <span>. So breadcrumbs contribute exactly one internal link (to Home) and must not be relied on for cluster/hub link flow — that is why layers 2–4 above matter more on this site than on a typical site.

6 • Anchor-text strategy

  • Descriptive, keyword-bearing anchors — use “BDS admission 2026”, not “click here” / “read more”. The anchor tells Google the target topic.
  • Vary the phrasing — for the BDS program page rotate anchors like “BDS course”, “Bachelor of Dental Surgery”, “BDS curriculum” across different source pages. Avoid the exact same anchor everywhere (looks manipulative).
  • Match anchor to target’s primary keyword — fees page anchored as “fee structure”, placements as “placement records”, each MDS spec by its branch name.
  • Keep anchors natural in the sentence — contextual links inside real sentences carry more weight than a bare list of links.
  • One canonical anchor concept per target — don’t anchor the same URL as both “fees” and “courses”; keep the topic consistent.

8 • Canonical-URL linking rules (do not break these)

Always link to the canonical URL — never to a URL that 301-redirects. Linking to a redirected URL wastes a hop and dilutes link equity.
Use this canonical targetNever link to (it redirects)
/admissions/ (and all /admissions/*)/admission/ & singular /admission/*
/academics/details-of-academic-programs/bds//bds/
/academics/details-of-academic-programs/mds//mds/
/fees-structure/ (plural)/fee-structure/ (singular)
  • Trailing slash on every internal URL — trailingSlash: true is set; /admissions redirects to /admissions/.
  • Use relative paths (/admissions/bds/), not absolute https://dental.jkkn.ac.in/…, for in-app <Link>.
  • Link only to pages that exist — the two /admissions/admission-process-guidelines/… nav links currently 404; replace, don’t link.

Bottom line. The site’s top-down crawl (mega menu) is healthy; the gaps are sideways & upward link flow. The three changes with the biggest ROI: (1) a Footer quick-links block, (2) putting the BDS/MDS/process pages in the Admission menu, and (3) the program↔blog↔admissions↔fees contextual bridges. Together they convert ~11 near-orphan money pages into well-linked, crawl-friendly, authority-fed pages.
