Comprehensive BDS Curriculum — DCI Approved Syllabus

A well-structured 5-year program covering all aspects of dental science and clinical practice as per DCI curriculum guidelines.

First Year (Pre-Clinical)

  • General Human Anatomy — Study of human body structure including gross anatomy, histology, and embryology with emphasis on head and neck.
  • General Human Physiology — Understanding body functions, organ systems, and their coordination essential for dental practice.
  • Biochemistry — Molecular basis of life processes, metabolism, and clinical applications in dentistry.
  • Dental Anatomy & Histology — Detailed study of tooth morphology, dental tissues, and oral structures.
  • Dental Materials — Introduction to materials used in dentistry, their properties, and applications.
  • Pre-Clinical Prosthodontics — Foundation course for artificial teeth and prosthetic devices.

Second Year (Para-Clinical)

  • General Pathology — Study of disease mechanisms, cellular pathology, and systemic pathological conditions.
  • General Microbiology — Bacteria, viruses, fungi, and their role in oral and systemic diseases.
  • Pharmacology — Drug actions, therapeutic applications, and prescription writing for dental practice.
  • Dental Pharmacology — Drugs specific to dentistry including local anesthetics, analgesics, and antibiotics.
  • Pre-Clinical Conservative — Foundation for tooth restoration and cavity preparation techniques.
  • Pre-Clinical Endodontics — Basics of root canal treatment procedures and techniques.

Third Year (Clinical)

  • Oral Pathology & Microbiology — Diseases affecting oral cavity, diagnostic pathology, and oral microbiome.
  • Oral Medicine & Radiology — Diagnosis of oral diseases, radiographic techniques, and interpretation.
  • General Surgery — Surgical principles, emergency management, and minor surgical procedures.
  • General Medicine — Systemic diseases and their oral manifestations, patient assessment.
  • Public Health Dentistry — Community oral health, epidemiology, and preventive dentistry.
  • Periodontology — Gum diseases, periodontal treatment, and maintenance therapy.

Final Year (Clinical)

  • Conservative & Endodontics — Advanced restorative procedures, root canal therapy, and aesthetic dentistry.
  • Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery — Extractions, minor oral surgeries, trauma management, and implantology basics.
  • Prosthodontics — Complete dentures, removable partial dentures, fixed prosthesis.
  • Orthodontics — Malocclusion diagnosis, treatment planning, and orthodontic appliances.
  • Pediatric Dentistry — Child dental care, behavior management, and preventive treatments.
  • Forensic Odontology — Dental identification, age estimation, and medico-legal aspects.

Internship (12 Months Rotational)

  • Comprehensive Clinical Training — Rotational posting across all dental departments with supervised patient care.
  • Community Dental Camps — Rural and urban outreach programs for community oral health promotion.
  • Emergency Dental Care — Training in dental emergencies, trauma management, and acute pain relief.
  • Research Methodology — Introduction to dental research, evidence-based dentistry, and scientific writing.
  • Practice Management — Clinic setup, patient management, ethics, and professional development.
  • Case Documentation — Maintaining complete case records, treatment planning, and follow-up protocols.
